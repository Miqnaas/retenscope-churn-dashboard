How to read the results

Recall 78.6% means the model found 294 out of 374 actual churners in the test data.

Precision 50.7% means many customers flagged as churners actually stayed.

The team must consider both when choosing a business threshold.

Research limitations

The dataset is a single historical snapshot. We cannot honestly claim to forecast churn within 30 or 60 days. Real-time prediction, model deployment and actual SHAP explanations are future integration work.
